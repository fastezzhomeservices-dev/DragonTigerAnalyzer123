# BIHAR SURVEY NON IT

Separate Android survey project based on the supplied legacy APK, Fathua_Section_DATA.xlsx and gggjkk.docx.

Implemented:
- Common login
- 9-category home screen
- Exact supplied Excel field sets for Building, Office Land, Office Equipment, Office Furniture and Residential Colony
- Office/location + section batch workflow
- Mandatory photo and GPS capture
- OCR and image-label suggestions
- Batch overview and confirmation
- Saved/rejected entry view
- XLSX export with category sheets and Latitude, Longitude, Photo Link, Item Identity, Survey Date and Survey Time columns

Vehicle, Plant & Machinery and Store Inventory are placeholders because their Excel field definitions were not supplied.
